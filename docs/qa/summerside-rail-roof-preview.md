# Summerside railway roof finish preview

Try the route on a local build by appending
`?departure=spinnakers-low&railShinglePreview=1` to the simulator URL.
Wait for **Summerside railway roof finish preview ready.** in Dispatch,
select **Begin flight**, and keep the camera in Chase. An early Begin
keeps the original station finish for that flight. **Pause → Choose
departure** allows a fresh ready launch even if you keep the same row.

The option changes the existing mapped pitched roof material, not the
roof shape, station or pavement. The original material and its
geometry remain the owner of the sorted draw. The temporary finish
borrows the same authored map and keeps the surrounding scene and
flight model unchanged. Standard still uses the original authored
station and roof. The separately documented stock station switch and
old shadow comparison remain independent; neither runs this finish.

The [two matched flight images](../../tools/art/summerside-station/roof-finish/evidence/ordinary-chase/)
show a restrained tone and clearer roof folds, not survey measurements
of current shingles. Independent native checks included the same
selected departure on a second launch and the original authored,
flat-stock and shadow choices. The material stayed stock after
a healthy module arrived too late for a trusted early Begin. It
also stayed stock on a real zero-antialiasing context. This is not a
hardware performance benchmark or a claim about other buildings.

On the same final build, separate ordinary Chase starts verified East
Point's two roof caps, the Summerside finish, and Basin Head's cannery
finish. Each started only its own preview. Requesting the East Point and
Summerside previews together started neither and fetched neither module.
