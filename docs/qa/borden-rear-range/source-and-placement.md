# Port Borden Rear Range Light: source and placement admission
This is the relocated decommissioned rear-range tower in Marine Rail Park, not the front or pier light. The PEI Lighthouse Society's prose describes the 12.8 m overall tower and its gallery, while a technical datum lists 11.6 m and should not be treated as the combined spire. The society's printed position disagrees by about 790 m with the current park location. No current-position authority is taken from that coordinate or from a photograph's camera coordinate.

## What qualifies
- The [PEI park opening announcement](https://archivenews.princeedwardisland.ca/park-pays-tribute-years-marine-and-rail-service) records the tower-and-observation-deck site at the former Marine Atlantic compound.
- The [Lighthouse Society's exterior record](https://peilighthousesociety.ca/index.php/lighthouses/range-lights/80-port-borden-back-range-light), [dated Dennis G. Jarvis views](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Port_Borden_Lighthouses), and [Dan Bhentschel's 2017 north view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PEI_Port_Borden_Back_Range_Lighthouse.jpg) establish the tapered white timber shaft, small skirted gallery, red lantern/cap, bracing and opening characteristics sufficient for original detached work. Reference pixels are not implementation assets. Details, material wear, position of local art faces and lower component dimensions are not surveyed.
- The [town's gallery](https://www.borden-carleton.ca/copy-of-borden-carleton-regional-library) corroborates open timber frames skirting both sides of the ramp in images labelled June 2023. Those images form one session, not reciprocal surveys or proof about 2020. The actual ramp, frames, signs, nearby building and park are not part of this tower asset.

## Why the live map remains unchanged
The signed 2020 NRCan building owner q0300/166 is a complex multi-lobe placeholder, not a footprint of the tower alone. An independent provincial building outline overlaps about 17.63 m² of the original model's 126.15 m² surveyed projection; approximately 108.51 m² of old plan lies outside the overlap. A separate bounded [2020 NRCan lidar](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/7069387e-9986-4297-9f55-0288e9676947) extent found multiple same-survey scan-source returns well above local grade in both lower lobes. They cannot be removed as empty, nor do aggregated point returns uniquely identify an opaque roof, solid building, or a present-day deck. The 2020 UPEI orthophoto was viewed privately as a dated visual check. It does not grant reuse, establish topology or provide texture pixels for the game.

A 2020 gross shaft orientation study did not reach its preselected held-track coverage. It provides no quantitative shaft widths or shape promoted to the asset. The intended 12.8 m exterior is a dated photographic interpretation, not a fitted footprint or engineering model. Exact insertion requires independent lower geometry, a stable visual/contact datum at the actual footprint, no terrain/road/shore edits, preserved neighbouring stock and a live moving acceptance flight. The standard Confederation Bridge departure is not such evidence: it flies away from this side of the park.
