# O’Leary Railway Station: horizontal owner check

**Result:** identify the surviving station’s original game building as **q20213/431**, not the nearest building to its civic point. This is like-for-like owner identification only. It does not verify a new asset, terrain height, current parcel ownership or a measured reconstruction.

The [Canadian Register](https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=20441) identifies the 1913–14 single-storey station beside the former railway, now the Confederation Trail, and describes the broad hipped canopy, brackets, station agent’s bay and covered platforms. Its photographs credited to C. Stewart (2013) establish recognizable north/east and west exterior forms without measuring dimensions. The [final 2015 Royal Gazette notice](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/publications/royal_gazette/20150314.pdf) names 513 Main and PID 909747. This historic identifier is correct for that notice but the current 909747 parcel does not cover a single building polygon.

The [provincial civic record, OBJECTID 56725](https://gis.princeedwardisland.ca/server/rest/services/civic_addresses/FeatureServer/0/query?where=OBJECTID%3D56725&outFields=OBJECTID%2CSTREET_NO%2CSTREET_NM%2CCOMM_NM%2CPID%2CO_PID%2Cpid2%2CINFO&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=geojson) explicitly links the old PID 909747 to current PID **1103332** for the same address. The [public cadastral geometry](https://gis.princeedwardisland.ca/server/rest/services/property_parcels/FeatureServer/0) makes the local differences measurable. The civic point is inside 1103332, about 1.83 m from 909747 and 43.01 m from the actual stock station footprint. The civic record says multiple accesses/buildings. We did not use it as a roof coordinate, and do not infer the date or legal implications of any change to cadastral boundaries.

**Independent controls:** the current parcel contains exactly one polygon in the original game and one provincial building outline, OBJECTID 12804. Their areas are 195.94 m² and 219.95 m² with 0.8908 intersection over union. The [2017 dated Larry photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Converted_Train_Station,_O%27Leary,_PEI_(35416486124).jpg) shows the hipped station next to the trail, with bins/elevator on its side and a distinct long metal building across. The [2020 georectified PEI orthophoto](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=2e9d3e4ce0c548a19cba5327607a4754) corroborates these relationships as a visual identity check. Neither image was traced or used as a texture. A nearby small OSM structure and the opposite-side stock polygon 256 are separate; the closer street-facing 430 and 395 outlines are the 505 Co-op building/canopy. [Veterans Affairs Canada’s cenotaph](https://veterans.gc.ca/en/remembrance/memorials/canada/oleary-cenotaph) is a separate object. Its registered point and the OSM memorial pin are about 24 m apart; neither is a building anchor. Protect both rather than choosing one as exact.

The [positive owner lock](independent-horizontal-owner.json) records the actual packed tile, row and footprint hashes, original horizontal ring, projection and neighboring clearances. It gives the game centre **X −78,729.8561 / Z −23,078.1970**, on the original base tile predicted horizontally as **3-7**. Arcade game metre comparisons are not a cadastral survey.

**Hold before placement:** no swap unless the authenticated original owner range and stock-rendered N112 terrain show that every new support, platform edge and eave stays inside the signed row-431 envelope and maintains road, trail, sibling and memorial clearances. If this cannot be established, leave the original render complete. No new survey elevations or geographic data were applied.
