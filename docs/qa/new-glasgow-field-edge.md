# New Glasgow field-edge pass

In **Dispatch**, choose **New Glasgow · field-edge pass** and **Begin
flight**. Or add `?departure=new-glasgow-field-edge` to the simulator's
URL. No scenery switch is required. The low pass starts at 225° and
42 metres above the starting terrain, southwest-bound across the
farmland north of New Glasgow in Queens County. This is a virtual
sightseeing route, not real-world navigation guidance.

The departure itself uses the released terrain, roads,
vegetation, camera and flight dynamics. The existing default and
other route entries keep their ordering. The
[mapped hedgerow artwork study](../../tools/art/hedgerow-bands/README.md)
did not replace the original hedges or their shadows. One nearby
[roof finish](new-glasgow-roof-tone.md) uses a dated overhead colour
reference; the original roof outline, height and walls are unchanged.

The [ordinary browser flight receipt](new-glasgow-field-edge.json)
records a real Dispatch click followed by three neutral-control
Chase-view checks at +233.272 m, +348.846 m and +711.105 m. The stock
terrain, roads, buildings and hedges remained active, with no page
errors or off-site requests. Chromium's software WebGL was used for
verification; this does not measure frame rate on a player’s hardware.
The flight also passes the checked-in terrain clearance test.
