# New Glasgow River Clyde pass

In **Dispatch**, choose **New Glasgow · River Clyde pass** or add
`?departure=new-glasgow-river-clyde` to the simulator URL. Start
58 metres above the local starting terrain, on a 285° heading. In
Chase, look past the right side of the aircraft as you cross the river
toward the Mill. This is virtual sightseeing, not real-world
navigation guidance.

This route uses the ordinary terrain, water, building atlas, controls,
and collision system. It does not replace the Mill. The
[Mill art workbench](../../tools/art/new-glasgow-mill/README.md)
shows the separate interpretive river-facing model and explains why it
has not been placed in the game. The original field-edge departure and
other Dispatch entries keep their order.

## Native flight check

An unmodified production build was flown from Dispatch with a real browser
click and the default Chase camera. The flight stayed in the selected departure
through 207.6 m, 368.0 m and 971.8 m of travel. Roads and buildings were on;
all four hedgerow sectors were ready by the first window. Six responses used
HTTP 206 byte ranges. The run reported no off-site requests or browser errors.
See the [native flight receipt](new-glasgow-river-clyde.json). This checks the
route and streaming, not the accuracy of the Mill or other scenery.
