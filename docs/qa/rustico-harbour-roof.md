# North Rustico harbour roof tone

The Standard North Rustico Harbour Low Pass gives one otherwise-brown roof in the
harbour an original pale-neutral tint. This is the existing four-triangle gable
of building-atlas tile `q03330`, row `377`, not the lighthouse, the separately
reviewed roof at row `268`, or the adjacent restaurant footprint.

- **Evidence:** [PEI 2020 aerial orthophoto viewer](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=2e9d3e4ce0c548a19cba5327607a4754) shows this roof as broadly pale. The two registered Web Mercator 256px tiles are z18 / y92781 / x84982, x84983 (SHA-256 `4169504fc5938b3ebeb02f6e8d3165c0a04ef6996b85063e18f306e9ea87f681`, `8b334c9a8126484acb83d5be763248926ff6466d1c80915f50a43e5781ca9894`). Provincial building footprint 125581 corroborates 89.72% of the federal owner outline in EPSG:3979; it is not a surveyed match for the whole exterior. The 2013 oblique in [Canadian Hydrographic Service ATL 108](https://waves-vagues.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/library-bibliotheque/chs-shc-ATL108-eng-202408-41254697.pdf), p. 1-30, is historical harbour context only.
- **Art:** `#aeb1aa` is an original illustrative sRGB swatch, not a pixel sample. No aerial or photo pixels are shipped or sampled at runtime. The stock shader, ridge, fascias, walls, terrain and flight are unchanged. The study does not identify any tenant, material, current paint or surveyed dimensions, and it does not add a new building.
- **Custody:** The exact atlas, native tile extent and compressed chunk, 674-row roster, and ordered q377 footprint and height fields must agree. The existing reader hashes the same 29,361 bytes once on that tile (shared with any /268 study) and makes no extra request. A missing/failed proof leaves the ordinary opaque roof. Only 12 pre-existing colour tuples are written differently; no new material, programs, passes, draw calls, buffers or frame work.
- **Compare:** Start at `?departure=north-rustico-harbour-low` and repeat with `&stockRusticoHarbourRoof`. Both accept the separate `rusticoMassingReview` option without changing it. Diagnostics report `rusticoHarbourRoof` for a mounted authored tile, not the roof's visibility in the camera. Detail or incompatible review settings keep the stock colour.
- **CPU witness:** `node --experimental-strip-types --test test/rustico-harbour-roof.test.ts` exercises both real exported Three building layouts, exact sibling/shape preservation, range/digest count, source faults, streaming cancellation and parser isolation. Ordinary roof colour range is [25128, 25140); with /268 mounted it is [25245, 25257). These ranges are observations, not runtime selectors.
