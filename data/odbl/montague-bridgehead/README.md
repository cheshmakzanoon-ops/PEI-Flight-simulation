This source subset records one public building way (652507973) and an exact
consecutive three-node portion of Main Street (208121569). Its geographic
coordinates are WGS84 [longitude, latitude], and ring closure is implicit.
The building way helps identify the footprint in the independent NRCan
atlas; the line selects its street-facing edge. Neither is rendered as
standalone geometry, and the larger OSM outline does not extend the existing
NRCan footprint used for the replacement. No OSM tags, private data,
contributor identifiers or imagery are included.
