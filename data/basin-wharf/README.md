# Basin Head outer wharf sources

The [OpenStreetMap way 361588794](../odbl/basin-head-channel/wharf-way.json)
is from the pinned Geofabrik extract, © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
The 2015 outline sets the site and station orientation, not the exact edge.
The main floor and channel-facing lip use the independent 2020 native
upper-shelf versus low-toe returns. Some measured reaches sit outside the
older generalized mapped outline.

The [scoped 2020 survey receipt](lidar-window.json) pins the provider object,
Byte Range source and decoded row hash. It comes from Natural Resources
Canada's [LiDAR Point Clouds, CanElevation Series](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/7069387e-9986-4297-9f55-0288e9676947)
and contains information under the [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
Class 2 returns record the upper surface in this crop; they do not certify
construction details. The date, elevation record and physical dimensions
used for the crop are not an as-built plan or current site survey.

The [PEI Museum and Heritage Foundation report](https://www.peimuseum.ca/sites/www.peimuseum.ca/files/2022-2023%20Annual%20Report%20FINAL%20-%20digital%20version.pdf)
(p. 9) shows a wooden boardwalk next to the Cannery.
The government's [post-Fiona field notes](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/publications/post_tropical_storm_fiona_highwater_mark_and_shoreline_erosion.pdf)
(p. 92) show vertical waterfront facing. Those photographs inform original
materials only. Neither photographs nor textures sampled from them are
included. The model does not recreate the newer, upstream stone groynes.
