# Basin Head Cannery source notes

The building is [Canadian Register of Historic Places entry 7436](https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=7436).
The original description and illustrations establish the asymmetrical
gable, paired dormers, five waterside doors, small cupola, timber and
shore additions. The pale weathered treatment is original artwork
informed by the [Foundation's view published by the National Trust](https://nationaltrustcanada.ca/online-stories/sun-salt-and-shorelines-discovering-the-spirit-of-summer-at-canadas-passport-places).
The photo's capture date is not asserted. No copyrighted photography,
labels, or depicted people are distributed in this project.

The plan reference is [the pinned OpenStreetMap way witness](../odbl/basin-head-channel/cannery-way.json),
which records the local envelope rather than a verified floor plan.
The [scoped PEI 2020 cloud](lidar-window.json) and the original 1 m
[HRDEM](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/0fe65119-e96e-4a57-8bfe-9d9245fba06b)
were used for roof and dry-side grade. This contains information
licensed under the [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
The original OpenStreetMap derivative has its own ODbL attribution.
