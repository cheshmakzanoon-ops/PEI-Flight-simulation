# Basin Head footbridge sources

The vertical fit uses the [Natural Resources Canada CanElevation LiDAR
point-cloud series](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/7069387e-9986-4297-9f55-0288e9676947),
specifically the PEI 2020 acquisition and a small, [pinned range-decoded window](native-receipt.json).
Contains information licensed under the [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
That input is orthometric elevation, not a measurement of navigable depth
below a chart datum.

The [separate OpenStreetMap way witness](../odbl/basin-head-channel/footbridge-way.json)
establishes the mapped route and registration, not the structural extent.
The deck uses the smaller extent supported by the point cloud. Its view
is informed by [Stefan Krasowski's dated 2012 photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Prince_Edward_Island_012_(7893580732).jpg)
([CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)). No imagery,
people, signs or photographic pixels are in this release.
