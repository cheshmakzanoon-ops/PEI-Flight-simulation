# Summerside northern court and Baywalk connector

This optional source-scoped development covers the approximately 95 × 111 m formerly water-masked block north of Spinnakers’ Landing, one raised timber connector, and two joined segments of an existing National Road Network road. It does not model the rest of the waterfront or any routes elsewhere in Summerside.

The [separately licensed directed coastline and way](../odbl/spinnakers/north-baywalk.json) are adapted from OpenStreetMap. The official [PEI 2020 point-cloud object](https://canelevation-lidar-point-clouds.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/pointclouds_nuagespoints/PEI/Prince_Edward_Island_2020/pei_2020_339000_704000.copc.laz) from Natural Resources Canada supplies corroborating stable class-2 ground and class-17 ramp elevations. The simulator keeps its existing fixed water elevation. As a conservative condition, neither a native class-9 water return nor a class-2 observation below +0.20 m can become dry. The high-density public data are not redistributed here.

The modern mapped shoreline alone is not sufficient. The complete stock-water/mapped-land pocket is naturally delimited by two actual contour intersections. A connected court uses only source-signed land with land-return support and a reserve from each low/water observation. The new cap meets ordinary ground at the real, currently submitted province faces. Local grade only changes on the restored court. The renderer uses a neutral authored dry aggregate palette because the inherited satellite tile carries water color at this location. It neither relabels today's parcel surfaces nor lays new pavement outside the registered road. The flight/contact height eases onto the stock height at the outer join; the rendered shape itself follows the active Three.js triangles throughout. The downloaded [receipt](native-scope.json) preserves the archive fingerprints and records the unquantized data-stage witnesses.

The northern wood way (OSM **1455214026**) and separately mapped sidewalk node anchor the short connector; published PEI Trails FID 156 independently corroborates the boardwalk's gross path. The rendered deck joins the actual existing Spinnakers’ Landing perimeter, not the nominal centreline endpoint. This 2020 point survey supports the descending grade, but the modeled width, rails, visible timber supports and finish are interpretations. The paved row uses the project's already licensed NRN data, pinned decoded record **2803**, segments 2 and 3. Transition pads meet the existing rendered road sections; no other road lengths, lanes, lines, markings or traffic are inferred.

Development flag: `?spinnakersPreview&northBaywalkPreview`. The court requires the pinned decoded land, elevation and road data, the exact 20,163-byte waterfront building tile and its two registered records, and a compatible deck. An unrelated tile change does not alter those local checks. Baseline and ordinary Spinnakers’ preview remain unchanged when not enabled. Do not use this model for navigation, access, tides, construction, or accessibility conclusions.
