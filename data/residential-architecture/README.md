# House roof survey windows

Published independently of the [ODbL matching database](../odbl/residential/).
Natural Resources Canada 2020 PEI building outlines and 1 m native
DSM/DTM are under the
[Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
Context can be screened with the Province of PEI's 2020 Corporate Land
Use Inventory under the
[Open Government Licence – Prince Edward Island](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/en/information/finance/open-government-licence-prince-edward-island).

The canonical native evidence file is sorted by exact atlas chunk and
original feature index. It contains shape model family, the SHA-256 of
the two audited source raster windows, ground datum, training and
holdout counts and errors. It contains no source point-cloud returns,
address, occupants or property owner information. It is not loaded by
the game. The fit reports shape relative to a survey ground point;
absolute scenery altitude follows the existing simulator terrain
anchor and is not a new bare-earth model.

See [housing scenery](../../docs/qa/residential-envelopes.md) for
selected coverage, reproducibility, exact limitations and art sources.
