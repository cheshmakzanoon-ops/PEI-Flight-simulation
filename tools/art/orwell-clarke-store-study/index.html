D.E. Clarke’s General Store

Orwell Corner, Queens County · detached study

Original, normalized study of the documented shopfront: large glazed displays, a divided transom above the recessed entrance, light clapboards, dressed pilasters and Island sandstone. The historical shop name is re-lettered, not traced. This is not a live flight model, an interior, or a survey of the roof and attached dwelling. Sources and placement limits
