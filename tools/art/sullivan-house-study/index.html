Sullivan House · the canted dormer

St. Peters Bay, Kings County · detached art

Second Empire-influenced house, built in the 1870s. This original proportional study separates three real sash planes, a peaked shingled hood, and a flared mansard/apron. It is not the building’s surveyed façade, guest accommodation, landscaping, or a live flight asset. Sources and scope
