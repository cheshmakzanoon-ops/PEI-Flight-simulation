# Tignish nave · two side joins

**Detached roof evidence only.** Two 1.84 m strips bridge the gap between
the earlier north and south side panels. They add 21.77 m² in the local
survey recipe and four upward-facing triangles. The four corners on
each strip come directly from the earlier west and east plane edges;
neither strip’s observations were used to refit its surface. The
crown, shoulders, tower, spire, exterior wall and grade gaps remain open.

The [source and comparison records](../../../../data/tignish-nave-mosaic-study/observations.json)
identify the exact [Natural Resources Canada CanElevation](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/7069387e-9986-4297-9f55-0288e9676947)
point cloud, data coordinate bases and raw first-return method. Checks
used new, frozen horizontal windows in the **same tile from the PEI
2020 collection** as the earlier fit. Its GPS-week values do not date
individual returns. This was not a separate survey or present-day verification. Only unique
classified building first returns were counted. The mesh comparison
uses the source orthometric heights directly, without a height offset.

A paired HRDEM 1 m mosaic provides an additional diagnostic. Its four
earlier sloped-panel windows give a local 0.337 m median bias. After
accounting for that in the mosaic comparison only, 12 and 10 cell
centres agree with the joins. Each strip contains just two complete
mosaic pixel squares, which is insufficient by itself; the mesh refuses
mosaic-only evidence. The bias is not transferred to other roof roles.

The stock game roof is unchanged and would still block both joins from
the tested approach. No site model, current structure dimensions,
terrain, collision or scenery release are authorized by this check.
