# Provenance and confidence

## Identity and orientation

- [Montgomery Sisam’s project record](https://www.montgomerysisam.com/project/indian-river-festival-pavilion/) identifies a permanent single-storey 2015 building on the church’s west lawn, roughly 1,600 ft², with a higher partial clerestory, northeast butterfly fall, wood deck, glulam and a steel valley beam. Its text repeats east for both transparent and mostly opaque groups; that duplicate direction is **not** used.
- [St. Mary’s venue/architect retrospective and construction photographs](https://underthespire.ca/something-old-something-new-david-sisam-on-designing-st-marys-pavilion/) corroborate the former west lawn tent site, original white cedar shingles, sealed spruce framing, clerestory, and the completed geometry relative to the historical church.
- [Original engineer presentation, Blackwell, 2019, PDF pp.38–39](https://www.forum-holzbau.ch/pdf/wbn19_Bowick_Hilchie.pdf) shows the original four labeled elevations and general valley framing. [WoodWorks Atlantic’s award record](https://atlanticwoodworks.ca/wood-design-awards-winners/) confirms the south/west glass sides and provides material context. These documents and architect/venue photos were inspected privately for factual design and orientation only. No drawing tracing, photograph, family member, attendee, article imagery or pixel snippets are part of this artifact. The north/east façade openings and finish are modeled descriptions, not present-day operations.

## Numerical source

[NRCan airborne LiDAR point clouds](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/7069387e-9986-4297-9f55-0288e9676947). PEI July–September 2020, bounded EPSG:2961 crop of [official COPC tile pei_2020_348000_714000](https://canelevation-lidar-point-clouds.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/pointclouds_nuagespoints/PEI/Prince_Edward_Island_2020/pei_2020_348000_714000.copc.laz). CGVD2013 orthometric Z. Native crop receipt gives exact header, bounds, ETag, point columns and digest. Input data/metadata are separately attributed under the Open Government Licence – Canada. The archive contains the derived numeric coefficients, witness subset and receipts, not the complete raw native data.

All 1,207 in-owner classified roof returns are sourceID 40511 from the same flight line. The five train/test splits are assigned only by 1.23 m XY blocks; there is no evaluation-point rejection by error or by observed height. The frozen output uses fold 0 training and one fixed XY plan-classifier. Interior means at least 0.38 m from a plan/step edge. Full-ring p90 of five held folds .028–.059 m. The modeled step cut varied `u=-3.133..-2.800` and `v=-2.975..-2.875` m across folds; that instability, and 134 corroborated returns outside the atlas outline out to 0.474 m, are why this is not an exact eave, exact wall or current as-built reproduction. We do not inflate the group to fit those exterior returns. Main slopes ~20.27% grid-local east and ~20.06% grid-local north; one real slope grows into the other like a valley, not a hipped stock box. The model does not infer precise historic junction trim from isolated edge returns.

Direct low Class 1 and Class 2 return strips (west n=116, south n=86) indicate medians 23.361 and 23.355 m respectively at the timber plate. Source assignment of individual Class 2 returns may include the wood deck and cannot be used as bare-earth truth. Authoring depicts the documented wood approach and conservative five broad courses tapering toward observed 22.7 m along the nearest lawn-looking toe; exact count, bottom and present access are illustrative. Studio grade is not an observation. Architectural window/door sizes, wood texture, stage lights, interior blank floor, upper/lower soffit thickness and glazing reflection are custom modeled. No interiors furnished or modern commercial fixtures added.

## Geographic exclusion

No game edit, property publication, modified mapping service, or live collision surface. A parallel source/real terrain oversight found the current church-specific V7 grade excludes this pavilion, q301 is a separate stock emitter, and replacing it would require grade considerably outside the originally accepted church contact. See the copied limited `existing-seat-scout.json` for the numeric mismatch. Bare-earth and real approach still need their own graded protection and current developer acceptance. Raised platform underside should **not** be interpreted as required soil contact, either on the real site or in a future renderer.

The retained skin is split into model-relative floor y=23.365, platform top 23.361, only the nominal slab underside 23.203, and a lowest authored step top/bottom 22.785/22.625 on the approach. No number/location assigned to hidden piers or permanent foundations. Stock geometry remains exact and is used only as the detached comparator.
