# Province House: detached south exterior study

Original Three.js / TypeScript model and material test, **not** a registered building, exact modern survey, or a production simulator update. It shows only the public-facing architectural envelope. The back and neutral foreground are deliberately generic. No photos, plan graphics, external texture files, interiors, site/access system, collision, elevation or ground modifications are served by the viewer.

## Reproduce

1. Install the pinned Node dependencies (`npm ci`, Node 22.12 or later).
2. `npm run build` runs strict TypeScript and an actual production Vite build.
3. With Python 3 and `numpy`, `Pillow`, `playwright`, run `python render.py`. It serves only the built `dist/` on loopback and captures real WebGL. Set `CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE` to an installed headless Chromium binary, or use a Chromium browser installed by Playwright.

`proof/report.json` records the exact run at 1096 × 680 with 4 samples. `proof/portico-study.webp` shows the unworked comparison shape, the exterior artwork at the same fixed eye, the closer view of the arcade, and a detail of the original sculptural capital. “Unworked” is this study's simple control, **not** the simulator's stock building. The gallery and compiled response are generated artwork, not donor images.

## Exterior evidence

- [Parks Canada heritage character statement](https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=580) and [Great George Street district](https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?i=65813&id=579): shallow hipped roof, Ionic columns above a more solid arched base, distinct rustic courses, smooth sandstone ashlar, projecting porticos and balanced wings. [Conservation timeline](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/provincehouse/conservation/calendrier-timeline) distinguishes the original competition sketch's flush entrances from the projecting built form adopted in 1844.
- [Peter Rukavina, 13 October 2026](https://ruk.ca/content/province-house-no-fence) and [Thelma Phillips, south exterior photographed 22 October 2026](https://thelmaphillips.ca/2026/10/23/legoislature/): two original current exterior observations. Each shows the four smooth, tapering drum shafts, lower triple segmental arcade with a wider centre, two glazing scales, low slate and masonry patchwork. They corroborate the south architectural character, not geodetic grade, engineering or an exact surface fit. Reference images were viewed but no pixels or image transformations are in the art or server bundle. The province [confirmed the October 2026 reopening](https://www.assembly.pe.ca/province-house-grand-reopening-events).
- [National Historic Site Management Plan](https://parks.canada.ca/-/media/lhn-nhs/pe/provincehouse/pd-mp_e.pdf?hash=62848A9E7BF717B55F89B5AEFE349A9AE214E552&la=en&modified=20171110165248), ©2005, p.14, Map 4 outer profiles only: nominal 10 m graphic bar spans 78.706 document points. Approximate shoulder/pavilion width 42.6 m, body depth 16.4 m, south plinth width 12.7 m and projection 3.2 m are a **pre-conservation printed-plan comparison**, not field dimensions. Generated envelope proportions are nominal, with no copied linework.
- [PEI GeoLinc published building footprints](https://gis.princeedwardisland.ca/server/rest/services/GeoLincData/Building_Footprints_2026/MapServer/0), [OpenStreetMap way 28325869](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/28325869) and [UPEI GeoREACH georectified 2000 provincial aerial](https://tiles.arcgis.com/tiles/fRRBCludvuolCm5B/arcgis/rest/services/Ortho2000_Mosaic_3857_L20/MapServer) are independent **read-only checks**, not assets. Published GeoLinc row 139144 is about 857.55 m² in EPSG:3979, with no actual survey date or source fields filled. Broad-axis full extents differ 45.36 × 24.39 m (provincial published footprint) versus 46.36 × 26.92 m (current mapped polygon), physical center positions about 2.9 m apart along the long axis. They overlap 780.43 m² (IoU .7935). Map polygons are not identical wall/eave/step definitions. The 2000 aerial supports the alignment and projecting character, but optical blur, shadows and roof overhang prevent a sub-metre wall-face conclusion. None of their coordinates or raster pixels are in the preview geometry.
- The [2017 Assembly report](https://www.assembly.pe.ca/sites/www.assembly.pe.ca/files/2019-04/legislative-assembly-ar-2017.pdf) records removal of the old slate during early conservation; the [2020 Federal announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/parks-canada/news/2020/02/province-house-national-historic-site---conservation-project--phase-three-and-phase-four.html) places the 2020 native survey during active envelope works, and [Parks Canada](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/provincehouse/conservation/faq) documents the new slate and copper and Phase Two finish in 2022. Neither the legacy AEB perimeter nor a 2020 near-flat DSM should be fit as a permanent modern roof.

## Scope and checks

- Clear, extruded segmental arches and three physically recessed openings, beveled voussoirs and stone/timber inner surfaces. Four smooth tapered drums with authored shallow course joints, sculptural mirrored volutes and shallow hipped slate. Distinct sandstone roles (dressed, rusticated/plinth and turned profiles), original analytic reflected light, recessed muntins and real stone return geometry. Nominal model stair geometry has a shared .465 m top threshold; 24 real Three.js rays check apron, three step tops, seam and vestibule. This is not an accessibility measurement or a representation of the current ramp.
- Original periodic sRGB color, linear normal and roughness maps; exact periodic error below 1e-11. The production lab serves only HTML and its bundled JS. No remote imagery or browser service dependencies.
- Authored Hero (38,321 triangles) and Approach (12,953 triangles) have the same masonry shell, arcade and windows; only the number of carved/tapered-order details changes. The study switches to Approach beyond 20 m. Locked 37 m/70 m views stayed under 192 pixels per 745,280 with a channel difference greater than 16 versus Hero; optical difference mostly local to the column joints, broad shadows and capitals. This is triangle/draw evidence, not a hardware-FPS claim. The detached texture set still builds ~40.5 MiB RGBA input and ~54 MiB fully mipmapped, excluding transient arrays. Reduce/share/stream it before mounting real scenes.
- The architectural art is usable as a reference. Keep it detached until a public post-conservation measured exterior, high-quality independently registered control photographs or comparable corroboration resolves **current** coordinates, roof heights, and local grade. The unverified rear, new paving, accessibility rail and land seating are intentionally not inferred.
